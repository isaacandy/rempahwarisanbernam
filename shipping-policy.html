Shipping & Delivery Policy

Clear, transparent delivery terms and rates across Malaysia.

Effective Date: 16 February 2026 • Last Updated: 22 November 2026

At Rempah Warisan Bernam Sdn Bhd (Company Registration No: 202501052286), we take utmost pride in milling, formulating, and dispatching premium halal-certified heritage spices to culinary enthusiasts, commercial caterers, and food service partners. This Shipping & Delivery Policy provides complete transparency regarding order processing, transit times, shipping rates, and delivery procedures.

1. Service Area & Delivery Destinations

We deliver nationwide across all 13 states and 3 federal territories in Malaysia:

  • Peninsular Malaysia: Selangor, Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya, Perak, Penang, Kedah, Perlis, Negeri Sembilan, Melaka, Johor, Pahang, Terengganu, and Kelantan.
  • East Malaysia: Sabah, Sarawak, and the Federal Territory of Labuan.

Note: For international export inquiries outside Malaysia, please contact our Industrial Export division directly at info@rempahwarisanbernam.net.

2. Order Cutoff & Handling Time

  • Daily Order Cutoff Time: 12:00 PM (GMT+8 / Malaysian Standard Time), Monday through Friday.
  • Order Handling / Processing Time: 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 12:00 PM are queued for processing the same or next business day. Orders placed after 12:00 PM, on weekends, or during public holidays will be processed on the next business day.
  • All spice packages are sealed in food-grade, multi-barrier pouches and packed securely in protective outer cartons to prevent humidity, sunlight exposure, and transit impact.

3. Transit Times & Courier Partners

Once your order has been dispatched from our central production and packaging facility in Rawang / Selangor, estimated transit times are as follows:

  • Peninsular Malaysia: 2 to 3 business days via authorized domestic courier partners (Pos Laju, J&T Express, DHL eCommerce, or Ninja Van).
  • East Malaysia (Sabah & Sarawak): 3 to 5 business days via express air freight couriers.
  • Total Estimated Delivery Time: 3 to 7 business days from the moment order payment is confirmed.

4. Shipping Costs & Calculation

Shipping costs are calculated transparently based on total order weight, volume, and recipient destination:

  • Standard Retail Orders: Calculated during order checkout or confirmed directly via our instant WhatsApp ordering concierge prior to payment.
  • Free Shipping Offers: From time to time, promotional free shipping vouchers may be offered on minimum qualifying spend amounts within Peninsular Malaysia. Promotional free shipping applies only to qualifying standard courier packages.
  • Transparent Quoting: There are no hidden fees, fuel surcharges, or packing handling charges added to the agreed shipping fee.

5. Order Tracking & Delivery Confirmation

Upon parcel handover to our logistics carrier partner:

  • You will receive a tracking number and carrier tracking link via email and WhatsApp.
  • You may track the live transit milestone updates directly on the carrier's tracking portal.
  • If you do not receive a tracking number within 2 business days of order confirmation, please reach out to our support team with your order reference.

6. Accurate Delivery Addresses & Failed Delivery Attempts

To avoid delivery delays:

  • Please provide a complete street address, unit / building name, and correct 5-digit Malaysian postcode. Our checkout modal provides automated address search via OpenStreetMap to ensure accurate geolocation.
  • Couriers typically attempt delivery up to two (2) times before routing the parcel to the nearest courier depot or collection hub. Customers are responsible for retrieving held parcels within the carrier's specified timeframe.
  • If a shipment is returned to sender due to an incorrect or incomplete address provided by the customer, reshipment fees will apply.

7. Damaged, Lost, or Delayed Shipments

We take food packaging and courier handoff seriously:

  • Transit Delays: While we partner with reliable national couriers, occasional delays may occur due to adverse weather, festive peak seasons (e.g., Hari Raya, Chinese New Year, Deepavali), or remote regional transit. We appreciate your patience and will assist in following up with the carrier.
  • Damaged in Transit: In the rare event that your spice parcel arrives damaged or compromised, please refer to our Return & Refund Policy. Notify us within 7 calendar days with photos of the outer carton and damaged spice pouch for an immediate replacement or refund.
