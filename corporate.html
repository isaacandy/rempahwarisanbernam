Five Decades of Spice Craftsmanship

Established in 1972 in the agricultural heartland of Bernam, our enterprise grew from a dedicated traditional spice mill into Rempah Warisan Bernam Sdn. Bhd.—a full-fledged Halal spice manufacturer headquartered in Bukit Beruntung, Rawang, Selangor.

While mass-market spice producers often dilute blends with starch fillers or artificial colourants, we remain uncompromising—sourcing select whole spices and milling them at low temperatures to protect natural essential oils.

5-Stage Precision Production & Quality Protocol

STAGE 01
Raw Spice Grading

Incoming whole spices undergo strict inspection for low moisture, cleanliness, aroma potency, and aflatoxin safety.

STAGE 02
Cleaning & Pre-Crush

Removal of foreign matter followed by coarse mechanical pre-breaking to ensure uniform feed density.

STAGE 03
Air-Classified Milling

Precision stainless-steel pulverizing (80–320 mesh) monitored below 35°C to retain volatile essential oils.

STAGE 04
Vibrating Sieve & Mix

Automated multi-deck sifting and timed homogeneous blending, with full dry-purge sanitation between SKUs.

STAGE 05
Barrier Pack & Trace

Thermal-sealed moisture-barrier packaging stamped with ERP Work Order batch codes and expiry dates.

Batch-Level QC & Certificate of Analysis (COA)

Every production run undergoes mandatory post-mix texture, colour, moisture, and thermal-seal integrity verification. We provide full product specifications and COA documentation to support corporate QA audits and food manufacturing vendor onboarding.

Proprietary Recipe Protection & Traceability

Our Rawang facility utilizes digitized ERP Work Order routing and segmented kit-batching protocols—protecting our 1972 heritage formulations as well as our OEM clients’ confidential proprietary spice recipes.
